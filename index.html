Privacy Policy for Mements

Privacy Policy for Mements Last Updated: September 8, 2026 Mements ("Mements", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it is stored, and the choices available to you when you use the Mements iOS application ("App"). 1. Information We Collect Mements is designed to collect only the information necessary to provide account and community functionality. Account Information When you create or access a Mements account, we may collect: Your name. Your email address. Information necessary to authenticate your account. FCM Token for Push notifications. You may sign in using: Sign in with Apple. Sign in with Google. Mements uses Supabase for authentication and backend services. Depending on the sign-in method and information provided by the authentication provider, your name and email address may be received and stored for the purpose of creating and managing your Mements account. For Sign in with Apple, you may choose to use Apple's private relay email address instead of sharing your personal email address. 2. Comments and User-Generated Content Mements allows users to post comments on content available in the App. Comments may be associated with your Mements account while your account is active and may be visible to other users of the App. Mements does not currently allow users to upload images or videos. All images and videos displayed in Mements are uploaded or managed by Mements and are not directly uploaded by users. 3. How We Use Your Information We use the information we collect to: Create and manage your Mements account. Authenticate you when you sign in. Display your name or account information in connection with your activity within the App. Allow you to post and manage comments. Operate and maintain the App. FCM token for push notification sending. Protect the security and integrity of the App. Review and investigate reports of potentially harmful, malicious, inappropriate, or infringing content. Respond to support requests when necessary. Comply with applicable legal obligations. We do not use your personal information for targeted advertising. 4. Legal Basis for Processing Personal Information If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction where a legal basis for processing personal information is required, we process personal information on the following legal bases, as applicable: Performance of a Contract We process information such as your name, email address, and authentication information because it is necessary to create and maintain your Mements account and provide the features of the App that you request. Legitimate Interests We may process information where it is reasonably necessary for our legitimate interests, such as: Maintaining and improving the security of Mements. Preventing fraud, abuse, spam, and misuse of the App. Investigating reports of potentially harmful or prohibited content. Enforcing our Terms of Use and Community Guidelines. Protecting our users and the integrity of the App. Maintaining and improving the reliability and operation of the App. Where we rely on legitimate interests, we consider the impact on your privacy and use this basis only where our interests are not overridden by your rights and freedoms. The ICO recognizes legitimate interests as one of the lawful bases under the UK GDPR. Legal Obligations We may process personal information where necessary to comply with applicable laws, regulations, legal proceedings, or valid governmental requests. Consent Where required by applicable law, we may process personal information based on your consent. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw your consent as permitted by applicable law. 5. Advertising and Monetization Mements currently: Contains no advertisements. Is not monetized. Does not use advertising networks. Does not sell user information to advertisers. Does not use your personal information for targeted advertising. Mements also does not currently use third-party analytics services or advertising SDKs to track users across apps or websites. 6. Third-Party Services Mements uses third-party services necessary to provide authentication and backend functionality. Supabase Mements uses Supabase for authentication and backend services. Supabase processes and stores information necessary to authenticate users and operate the services used by Mements. When a user deletes their Mements account, Mements deletes the user's personal account data from its Supabase backend. No personal user account data is retained in Supabase after account deletion, except for anonymized comments as described in this Privacy Policy. Apple Mements uses Sign in with Apple to allow users to authenticate their accounts. Google Mements uses Google Sign-In to allow users to authenticate their accounts. These third-party services may process information according to their respective privacy policies and terms. We do not authorize these services to use Mements account information for advertising on our behalf. 7. International Data Transfers Mements and the third-party service providers we use may process or store personal information in countries other than the country in which you reside. This may include countries outside the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom. Where personal information is transferred internationally, we take appropriate measures required by applicable data protection laws to help ensure that your personal information receives an appropriate level of protection. Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, international transfers may rely on: An adequacy decision recognized under applicable data protection law; Appropriate contractual or other safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses or applicable UK transfer mechanisms; or Another lawful transfer mechanism permitted by applicable data protection law. European and UK data protection rules provide specific requirements and safeguards for international transfers of personal information. You may contact us using the information provided in this Privacy Policy if you would like additional information regarding applicable safeguards for international transfers. 8. Information Sharing We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share your personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. Information may be processed by service providers such as Supabase, Apple, and Google where necessary to provide authentication and backend functionality. We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to: Comply with applicable law or legal process. Protect the rights, safety, or property of Mements, our users, or others. Detect, prevent, or address fraud, security, or technical issues. Investigate reports of potentially harmful, malicious, inappropriate, or unlawful content or activity. 9. Data Storage and Security Your account information and other information necessary to operate Mements may be stored using Supabase's services. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. 10. Data Retention We retain your personal account information for as long as necessary to provide Mements' services and maintain your account. When you delete your account, your personal account data is deleted from our backend systems, including Supabase. This includes your: Name Email address Authentication/account information Profile picture Other personal information associated with your account Your previously posted comments may remain visible within the App after account deletion. These comments are anonymized to protect your privacy while preserving the community discussion. After account deletion: Your previous username is replaced with "Deleted Account". Your profile picture is removed. Your previously posted comments will also be deleted and will not be visible to anyone on the app. 11. Account Deletion Mements provides users with the ability to delete their account directly from within the App. You do not need to contact customer support to request account deletion. When you choose to delete your account: Your personal account information is deleted from Mements' backend systems, including Supabase. Your authentication/account information is deleted. Your profile picture is removed. Your previously posted comments will also be deleted and will not be visible to anyone on the app. Mements does not retain your personal account data after account deletion. Comments are retained solely to preserve existing community discussions and are displayed without your previous identifying profile information. 12. Users can submit a report directly through the App. Reported content may be reviewed and investigated by Mements. If, following our review, we determine that the content violates applicable laws, our Terms of Use, Community Guidelines, or otherwise contains malicious, harmful, inappropriate, or prohibited material, we may remove or take down the reported content and take other appropriate action. Depending on the circumstances, Mements may also take action against accounts responsible for violating our rules, including restricting or terminating access to the App. We may retain information reasonably necessary to investigate reports, enforce our policies, protect users, and prevent abuse or repeated violations. 13.
